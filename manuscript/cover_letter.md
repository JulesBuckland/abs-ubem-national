Dear Editors,

We submit our manuscript, "Scalable Bayesian Computational Framework for National Housing Stock Characterization: Decoupling Building Physics from Observed Energy Consumption," for consideration in *Energy and Buildings*.

This paper builds directly upon the methodological foundation established by Neto-Bradley, Choudhary, and Challenor (2023), extending their spatial stock estimation approach to a national UK context. We introduce the Empirical Thermal Index ($T^*$) as a behaviorally-adjusted characterization metric that addresses a critical methodological problem in urban energy governance: the systematic misallocation of retrofit capital driven by behavioral rationing in socioeconomically deprived areas.

By integrating administrative microdata across 6,840 Middle Layer Super Output Areas (MSOAs) via parallelized population synthesis and hierarchical spatial inference, we demonstrate that national-scale characterization of the "hidden cold" is computationally feasible. Our framework isolates the structural building performance from the noise of human behavioral response, providing a robust, ground-truthed tool for equitable Net Zero transitions.

Given the alignment with the methodological and empirical themes of recent research published in this journal, we believe our work will be of significant interest to the readership of *Energy and Buildings*.
